|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | anonymous | adj. | 익명의 | The donor asked to remain **anonymous**. |
| 2 | memorable | adj. | 기억에 남는 | The trip was a **memorable** experience for the whole family. |
| 3 | consumer | n. | 소비자 | Many **consumer** prefer to shop online because prices are lower. |
| 4 | competent | adj. | 유능한, 능숙한 | We need a **competent** accountant to manage our records. |

| 7 | motivated | adj. | 의욕 있는, 동기가 부여된 | Highly **motivated** employees tend to work harder. |
| 8 | satisfactory | adj. | (기준에) 만족스러운, 충분한 | The inspector found the repairs to be **satisfactory**. |
| 9 | available | adj. | 이용 가능한, 시간이 되는 | Tickets are **available** at the front desk. |
| 10 | safety | n. | 안전 | Workers must wear helmets for **safety** reasons. |
| 11 | loyal | adj. | 충성스러운, 단골의 | She is a **loyal** customer who has shopped here for years. |

| 13 | safely | adv. | 안전하게 | The package arrived **safely** at our office on Monday. |
| 14 | merger | n. | (기업) 합병 | The **merger** of the two banks was announced yesterday. |
| 15 | sustained | adj. | 지속적인 (오래 이어지는) | The company has achieved **sustained** growth for five years. |
| 16 | release | v. | 공개하다, 출시하다, 석방하다 | The company will **release** its new smartphone in March. |
| 17 | substantial | adj. | 상당한 (양·정도가 큰) | The new policy led to a **substantial** increase in sales. |
| 18 | observe | v. | 관찰하다, (규칙을) 지키다 | Employees must **observe** the company dress code at all times. |
| 19 | necessary | adj. | 필요한 | A valid passport is **necessary** to enter the country. |
| 20 | interfere | v. | 간섭하다, 방해하다 | Loud construction work may **interfere** with our ability to concentrate. |
| 21 | safe | adj. | 안전한 | Please keep the medicine in a cool, **safe** place. |
| 22 | satisfied | adj. | (사람이) 만족한 | We are very **satisfied** with the results of the test. |
| 23 | upcoming | adj. | 다가오는, 곧 있을 | Please submit your report before the **upcoming** board meeting. |
| 24 | improvement | n. | 개선, 향상 | The survey shows a clear **improvement** in customer service. |
