# 동하 TOEIC 오답 원문 (Test 5 = ETS 교재 pp.140-155 / 사진 IMG_1807~1810)
정답은 `ANS:`, 동하가 고른 답/상황은 `MINE:` (모르면 생략). 문제는 하나씩 내고 ANS는 숨길 것.

## PART 5
### T5-107 [어휘·연어]
Last weekend, Terriville Community Center held a ______ game night for neighborhood children.
(A) removable (B) plentiful (C) lively (D) current
ANS: C lively (활기찬 행사). MINE: plentiful 쪽으로 헷갈림. RULE: lively(활기찬 행사) vs plentiful(양이 풍부)

### T5-111 [어휘·연어]
Many farmers markets operate on a seasonal ______, opening in spring and closing in late autumn.
(A) topic (B) basis (C) root (D) sum
ANS: B basis. RULE: on a ~ basis (seasonal / regular / weekly basis)

### T5-126 [준동사 ing/ed]
Mr. Sato will call into the meeting ______ the hotel's wireless network.
(A) used (B) using (C) use (D) had used
ANS: B using. RULE: 동사 이미 있음 + 접속사 없음 + 뒤에 목적어(the hotel's wireless network) → 분사구문 -ing

### T5-127 [어휘]
Not only has Mr. Ogbu ______ on the project, but he has also trained Ms. Jeong to continue the work.
(A) accessibly (B) diligently (C) eventually (D) completely
ANS: B diligently (worked diligently). RULE: work diligently / finish completely

### T5-165 [Part 7, 동하 오답] notice "Important Information Regarding Employee Safety" (사진 IMG_1810)
Q165. What responsibility of a supervisor is mentioned in the notice?
(A) Distributing employee manuals (B) Overseeing the work of the safety coordinator (C) Regularly updating the posted safety rules (D) Providing safety guidelines for specific jobs
ANS: D. 근거: "Certain jobs require employees to follow the additional safety guidelines provided by their supervisors." MINE: C. 함정: posted safety rules는 지문에 있지만 supervisor가 하는 일이 아님(직원이 따르는 것).
### T5-166 [어휘 의미]
The word "measures" in paragraph 1, line 6, is closest in meaning to (A) amounts (B) procedures (C) dimensions (D) penalties
ANS: B procedures ("additional safety measures" = 추가 안전 절차). 동하 두 번 다 오답.
### T5-169 [Part 7, 동하 오답, 두 번 다 틀림] job description "HIGHWAY SUPERVISOR" (Dora County, 사진 IMG_1810)
Q169. According to the job description, what should the highway supervisor do if a serious problem with a crew member arises?
(A) Suggest a replacement worker. (B) Restrict the crew member's activities. (C) Notify the division director of the issue (D) Designate a liaison to explain the matter to management.
ANS: C. 근거: "the supervisor informs the division director of major personnel problems". 함정: liaison 단어가 지문에 있지만 그건 supervisor 자신이 liaison 역할 (역할 바꿔치기).
