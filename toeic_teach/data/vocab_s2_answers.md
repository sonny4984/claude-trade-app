# Answer key

## Set 1

| No. | English | P.O.S. | Korean | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 장소 명사, where + 절 | conj. (relative) | 그곳에서 ~, ~하는 (장소) | The conference will be held in Busan, **장소 명사, where + 절** the company was founded. |
| 2 | by + 시점 | prep. | ~까지는, 늦어도 (마감, 한 번에 끝내는 일) | Please submit the report **by + 시점** 4 p.m. today. |
| 3 | be accustomed to V-ing | adj. phr. | ~하는 데 익숙하다 (to는 전치사) | Most of our staff are **be accustomed to V-ing** working overtime in December. |
| 4 | intangible | adj. | 무형의, 만질 수 없는 | Good will is an **intangible** asset that is hard to measure in money. |
| 5 | go into effect | v. phr. | 효력이 발생하다, 시행되다 | The new parking rules will **go into effect** on Monday. |
| 6 | in order to V | phr. | ~하기 위해서 (목적, to 뒤는 동사원형) | **in order to V** save energy, the office turns off the lights at night. |
| 7 | tangible results | n. phr. | 구체적으로 확인되는 성과 (실체가 있는, 증거·숫자로 보이는) | The new training program has already produced **tangible results** in sales. |
| 8 | until + 시점 | prep. | ~까지 (그 시점까지 계속) | The store stays open **until + 시점** 9 p.m. on weekends. |
